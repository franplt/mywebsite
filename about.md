I'm Francesco Paltrinieri, Head of Marketing at TextYess. We build AI agents that run CRM for e-commerce brands.

I have spent almost ten years in startups across Europe, Asia, and the US. Before TextYess, I worked on product, AI, and growth at Builder.io in San Francisco, where I helped launch an AI-powered Figma-to-code tool. Before that, I worked on growth at Contentful in Berlin, at Everli in Milan, and at First Circle in the Philippines. I also co-founded Garisenda Lab, a direct-to-consumer e-commerce brand.

I studied finance at Bocconi and Ivey. I have lived and worked in Italy, Portugal, Germany, Spain, the Philippines, Canada, and the UK. Now I live in Lisbon.

On the side, I built Corso AI, an AI course in Italian for non-technical people.

I like data, SQL, clean design, and user interviews.
